## Egypt birth-dose detail

The current Egyptian routine table lists hepatitis B within 24 hours after birth, oral polio during the first week, and BCG at birth. It then lists oral polio, pentavalent, and IPV at 2, 4, and 6 months; oral polio at 9, 12, and 18 months; MMR at 12 and 18 months; and vitamin A at 6, 12, and 18 months. The app calculates each reminder from the entered date of birth and displays the timing window beside the date. Vitamin A is shown explicitly as a supplement, not a vaccine. The vaccine card at the local health center remains the source to follow.

The app also lists PCV, rotavirus, seasonal influenza, hepatitis A, varicella, and meningococcal vaccines as additional discussion items. They are not treated as fixed Ministry routine appointments: age limits, number of doses, intervals, eligibility, and availability depend on the product, the child's history, local policy, and the pediatrician's assessment. Each item includes a safety-information link and common/rare reaction guidance.

## Limits

Some entries are seasonal, regional, risk-based, or dependent on a child's birth cohort or vaccine product. The app marks known conditional entries and includes source links. The calendar is a reminder aid, not a complete clinical catch-up calculator. It does not decide eligibility, contraindications, or the next dose after a missed appointment. A severe allergic reaction, breathing difficulty, seizure, marked lethargy, persistent high fever, or an unusual high-pitched cry after vaccination requires urgent medical assessment.
