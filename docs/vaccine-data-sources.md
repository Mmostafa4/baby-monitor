# Vaccine reminder data sources

> Review status: the app's country tables are a simplified reminder draft. They need review by the relevant health authority or a qualified clinician before clinical use. Follow the child's official vaccine card and local health center.

Data was checked on 20 September 2026. The date is when the schedule pages were inspected; it is not the publication date of every country's schedule.

The app normalizes combined products into plain-language vaccine groups to avoid listing every brand or formulation as if it were a separate shot.

## Egypt birth-dose detail

The Ministry of Health's child/newborn guidance says the zero dose of hepatitis B should be given within 24 hours after birth and the zero dose of oral polio vaccine during the first week. The app displays those timing windows beside the birth date. The Ministry's child-vaccination page is linked inside the app; the vaccine card at the local health center remains the source to follow.

## Limits

Some entries are seasonal, regional, risk-based, or dependent on a child's birth cohort or vaccine product. The app marks known conditional entries and includes source links. The calendar is a reminder aid, not a complete clinical catch-up calculator. It does not decide eligibility, contraindications, or the next dose after a missed appointment.
